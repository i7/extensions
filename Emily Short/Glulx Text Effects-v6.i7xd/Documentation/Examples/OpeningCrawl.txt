Example: ** Opening Crawl - Use custom styles to write a full title page.

	*: "Space Wars Episode 4" by Georgia Lucifer.
	
	The story headline is "A Space Conflict".
	
	Outer space is a room.

	Include Glulx Text Effects by Emily Short.

	Grand title is a glulx text style.
	Subtitle is a glulx text style.
	Author byline is a glulx text style.
	Producer byline is a glulx text style.
	Opening crawl is a glulx text style.

	Table of User Styles (continued)
	style name	justification	relative size
	grand title	center-justified	15
	subtitle	center-justified	10
	author byline	center-justified	5
	producer byline	center-justified	-5
	opening crawl	center-justified

	When play begins:
		now the left hand status line is "";
		now right hand status line is "";
		say "[grand title letters]Space Wars Episode 4";
		say "[line break][subtitle letters]A Space Conflict";
		say "[paragraph break][author byline letters]Written and directed by Georgia Lucifer";
		say "[line break][producer byline letters]Produced by J J Bincs";
		say "[paragraph break][opening crawl letters]It is a period of stellar war.";
		say "Between the stars fly spaceships, and sometimes, space bombs.";
		say "The space senate fights back against the space empire.";
		say "Long the space emperor has sought after the space king's daughter,[line break]";
		say "but now it looks like she might be falling into his hands...[paragraph break]";
		pause the game;
